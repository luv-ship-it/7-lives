# 7-lives
 Welcome to the 7-lives, where you will be given choice to live as you want with each lives having its own struggle. Each lives are a stage to clear and beat the final boss.

This is a game, where you beat each level and at the final level the game is officially over and you lived all your lives.

But if you fail or something happens in certain lives, you reenter the live again and the loop contniues until you clear a specific lives.
